About / Made by

Mosaic is a scrapbook-style collage & poster maker that runs entirely in your browser. Photos are stored as original files in your browser's IndexedDB, right next to your project — nothing is uploaded, ever.

  • Layouts: grid, freeform, polaroid scatter, magazine cover
  • Canvas filters: vintage, duotone, film grain, B&W, warm, cool, faded
  • Washi tape, paper clips, doodles, text labels, frames & generated paper
  • Exports: PNG/JPEG up to A4 at 300 dpi and 4×6 in prints
  • Shortcuts: Ctrl+Z / Ctrl+Shift+Z, Ctrl+D duplicate, Delete, arrows to nudge, [ and ] to reorder, Esc to deselect

Why did the photo go to the scrapbook? It needed some space to develop.
